Jason,
I saw your post in which you said you were looking for projects in need of music (http://opengameart.org/forumtopic/composer-available-for-your-game-or-project).
Our project is known as The Secret Chronicles of Dr. M (abbreviated TSC).  We are a fork of a well known open source game, Secret Maryo Chronicles.  We changed the name of the game in this release in order to avoid obvious references to Mario.
As part of an effort to not be viewed as a Mario clone, we are overhauling Mario style graphics and music.  Unfortunately, we still have a number of Mario remixes in our music collection and do not yet have suitable replacements.  Our musicians are currently unavailable for composing.  This has been delaying our planned release, since we really need the first release under the new name to be free of major Mario references.

If you are able to compose new music (or have music you have already composed), it would greatly help us.
These are the songs we are needing replaced:
   
   - https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Secretchronicles/TSC/release-2.0.0/tsc/data/music/land/jungle_1.ogg - Super Mario World land level music
   - https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Secretchronicles/TSC/release-2.0.0/tsc/data/music/land/land_1.ogg - Super Mario World general map music
   - https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Secretchronicles/TSC/release-2.0.0/tsc/data/music/land/land_2.ogg - Super Mario World Vanilla Dome music
   - https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Secretchronicles/TSC/release-2.0.0/tsc/data/music/land/land_5.ogg - Super Mario 64 Bob-Omb Battlefield music (this is also our default music for new levels right now)
   - https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Secretchronicles/TSC/release-2.0.0/tsc/data/music/land/water_1.ogg - Super Mario Bros. Invincibility music
   - https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Secretchronicles/TSC/release-2.0.0/tsc/data/sounds/player/dead.ogg - Super Mario World player dying music

We will of course take additional music as long as it is something we can use.If you decide to submit music, please submit it at our forum at: http://forum.secretchronicles.de/forums.  This way there is no doubt we have permission to use it.
If you have any questions, let us know.
-Chris Jacobsen (datahead)
