Quintus's proposed vote seemed to imply a permanent decision without further changes.  People usually do not reverse such decisions, either.
I respect your general views on the piano, but if we are going to hold a vote, I think we should look at all options.  I also realize it seems like a lot of decisions for a small component in a song, but if we aren't going to make a decision based on all options, we should just accept the music as-is and be done with it.  This, however, defeats the purpose of critiquing songs or artwork.

>
> This is a formal call for vote on a topic regarding the game of TSC as
> per our voting guidelines layed out in the last General Discussion in
> IRC. Allowed to vote is every team member as listed on the project team
> page at <https://github.com/orgs/Secretchronicles/people>. You can vote by
> posting a single message containing the text “I vote” followed by the
> option you vote for. Each team member has only one vote. Winner option
> is the option that receives the most votes; if no single option receives
> the most votes, a second vote is taken with the set of options reduced
> to those options which received the most votes. The winner option of the
> second vote is deemed the winner of the entire voting process; if still
> no single option receives the most votes, the entire vote is failed and
> has no binding result. Further discussion is then required. The winner
> option is a binding decision that determines how to proceed.
>
> Any team member can request a covert vote if he feels it is necessary;
> the request has to be honoured if it is made. Refer to the announcement
> at
> <http://team.secretchronicles.de/~quintus/general-discussions/1st-general-discussion.txt>
> for the details of the covert voting process (section “VOTING
> PROCESS”). The request must be made before the voting period starts (if
> people already voted openly, a covert vote would not be able to
> guarantee anonymity).
